Your text stays yours.

Before anything reaches an AI provider, Jot redacts sensitive information on your device — then restores it locally after the rewrite. The model only ever sees masked text.

john@acme.com → [EMAIL_1]   415-555-0182 → [PHONE_1]

4. Restored on device

Please let john@acme.com know the refund was approved, but the account remains on hold. They can contact me at 415-555-0182 if they have any questions.

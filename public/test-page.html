Extension test page

Served over HTTP so Chrome's activeTab grant covers it when you invoke Jot. Open in Chrome with the unpacked extension loaded.

  1. Load the unpacked extension from .output/chrome-mv3 in chrome://extensions.
  2. Focus any field below, or click Select text to highlight its contents.
  3. Press Ctrl+Shift+Space (macOS: ⌘+Shift+Space) or click the Jot toolbar icon to open the overlay.
  4. Choose a style with ←/→, press Enter to rewrite, type a tweak and press Enter, then press Enter on the content box to insert it back into the field.
  5. Pressing the shortcut again closes the overlay and removes Jot from the page entirely.
